# Manufacturing operating rules

These rules retain the detailed arithmetic, bounds, record shapes and native-person authority behind the contract. Equivalent agent actions use the fleet risk classes and the company’s policy. Historical implementation limits in CHANGELOG.md are not approval defaults.

## ENG-002

A revision includes ordered operations, dependencies, setup/run/queue times, work-center eligibility, labor skills, drawings and step instructions. Operators see the exact approved revision at their task, including a required acknowledgment after an instruction change. Setup and queue estimates are minutes per job; run time is minutes per base-unit output (legacy `minutes` aliases run time), each finite in the inclusive range 0–100000. Dependencies name unique earlier operation sequence numbers, defaulting to the previous operation; an explicit empty list permits an independent step. Eligible center names match exactly. The production role and the GOV-001 engineer author routings, and production people and GOV-001 operators operate them; the owner or manager records a named active production person’s or operator’s skill authorization for the exact task before starting work that requires those skills. This is a human authorization, never certification. The assigned person and current native role are checked at execution; An agent’s equivalent call carries its computed risk classes and the company’s policy decides it; a person’s signature is recorded only after that person acts. Saving through the authorized engineering door records the authorizing person and time. Release snapshots instructions and original drawing files/digests, and each job starts with unacknowledged tasks. A person acknowledges that job’s exact pinned BOM before starting an instruction/drawing task; predecessor completion and eligible center selection are enforced through both tablet and command API. Future revision/intake doors in ENG-004, ENG-005, CONN-011, MOVE-001 through MOVE-006 and AGENT-005 inherit the same pinning and acknowledgment obligations before shipping.

## ENG-003

Sales/engineering choose permitted variant attributes, dimensions and options; rules derive component quantities, routing and cost with explainable formulas. Invalid combinations are refused and the resolved configuration is pinned on quote and work. A configuration model is a saved revision per made item : attributes with permitted values, dimensions with a unit and a range, yes/no options, rules whose conditions name attributes, options or dimension ranges and whose formulas derive component quantities per unit, routing minutes and added cost, and refused combinations with their reason; formulas are dry-run when saved. A choice resolves to its components, routing and cost, each figure carrying the rule and the arithmetic behind it. A quote line pins the resolved configuration; a work order drafted from that line copies it, and releasing it pins exactly those components, routing and cost with no active BOM; a later rule edit is a new revision and moves neither the quote nor released work, and a quote against a replaced revision is refused.

## ENG-005

Engineers propose changes with reason, effectivity, attachments, substitute eligibility and disposition of obsolete stock; authorized managers approve, record acknowledgment and choose treatment of unreleased work. Approved revision history is immutable. Production, a GOV-001 engineer or a manager prepares, and owner/manager approves an exact state version, with a different approver except the owner; engineers never approve. A pending safety change blocks new affected work releases; an open proposal also blocks direct and CSV BOM revision bypass. Each affected draft, including a parent that flattens a changed phantom, receives a reasoned keep-previous, adopt-approved or cancel decision. Adoption cannot release before the approved UTC effective date; keeping uses the explicitly reviewed previous revision. The releasing production person acknowledges the exact approved change before affected work releases; task instructions/drawings still require ENG-002’s job-level acknowledgment. Substitutes name the removed and replacement components with eligibility evidence. Every positive removed-component stock position has a quantity/location/lot/disposition instruction and reason; these instructions never claim a physical return, rework or scrap movement. Actual stock actions use their own authorized doors. Evidence files and drawing bytes/digests, source/prospective/approved revisions, decisions and acknowledgments remain in the archive. An intervening business write refuses approval; decline and propose a new review.

Propose a safety-related component replacement with an attachment and substitute eligibility, deny direct and phantom-parent release before approval and refuse BOM bypass, independently approve on an effective date, acknowledge it and inspect old stock instructions plus keep/adopt/cancel draft outcomes and unchanged released jobs.
Refuse premature adoption, stale/self/agent approval; restore the evidence and immutable review history, and require the new job’s pinned instruction acknowledgment.

## ENG-006

Sellable kits have versioned component definitions; an authorized sale can trigger policy-approved assembly or component picking. Stock, kit availability and costs reconcile once even when the channel repeats the sale event. A kit revision lists its components per kit and its policy (assemble the kit, or pick the components); a manager, production, engineer or agent prepares it and a different manager or production person (the owner excepted) approves the policy, which makes it the one active revision. An authorized sale (owner, manager or sales) names the channel, the channel's own event id, the quantity and the one location it draws from: the first sight of an event posts, in one atomic write, the exact components of the pinned revision (and, for an assembling policy, the kit assembled and sold at the components' cost), and refuses before anything posts when any component is short; a repeat of the same event under any command identity returns the recorded sale and moves nothing, and the same event with different content is refused. Kit availability is what each location can build or pick from available stock.

## ENG-007

An authorized manager, buyer or engineer downloads the active catalog of items and variants as XLSX or CSV, with the Larder ID and every editable field per row (per location where the field is location-specific): SKU, name, category, sales price and price list, purchase price, default supplier and supplier code, barcode/GTIN, reorder point, safety stock, min/max, default bin, batch/serial tracking, lead time and active/archived state. Re-uploading the edited file matches rows by Larder ID; rows without an ID are creates. An empty cell leaves the stored value unchanged and only an explicit clear marker removes a value. The preview lists each changed field (old and new value), each row error (file, row, column, reason) and what each change affects: open quotes and orders, planning policies, open POs and tracked stock. Approval applies all accepted rows atomically against the previewed state version. Used units, stock movements, reservations and issued documents are never rewritten, and turning on batch/serial tracking for an item with untracked stock is refused with its reason. CSV is the delivered format; the download includes every current editable item field and one row per active location, with locations serving as bins. Global edits on repeated location rows must agree, and an invalid row excludes its whole item group. Blank cells preserve values; #CLEAR removes an optional value, with a leading backslash escaping a literal #CLEAR (one added backslash escapes a preceding backslash run). Text exports carry one reversible leading apostrophe, preserving numeric identifiers, leading zeros and formula-like text. A new item's ID-less location rows group by SKU. Supplier terms retain their purchase unit in party/item references; catalog edits affect future documents and never reprice saved ones. Location reorder/safety/min/max/bin/lead settings are retained configuration for DEMAND-004; that clause must honor them before its policy planner ships. Manager/buyer/production and the GOV-001 engineer role export, prepare and approve catalog edits, with a different human approver except the owner. An agent’s equivalent call carries its computed risk classes and the company’s policy decides it; a person’s signature is recorded only after that person acts. Tracking selection is a declared catalog policy: batch requires a valid lot on current physical receipts/output, while serial is labelled “serial policy configured; one serial per unit at every stock door” and every physical movement of a serial item is one named serial unit through TRACE-002's doors; a door that cannot name serials refuses. Enabling batch is refused for any nonzero unlotted position across all locations/dispositions; serial selection is refused while any nonzero physical stock exists. TRACE-002 owns serial-aware execution and must honor the configured serial policy before shipping.

## DEMAND-002

Sales versions order quantities, dates, addresses and prices with customer approval history. Changes identify affected reservations, procurement, work and shipped balances; cancellation releases only unconsumed commitments and leaves already incurred costs visible. Customer approval is the authorized salesperson’s locally recorded attestation (name, source reference, received date and optional original evidence), never a claim of remote identity validation. An amendment preview retains old/proposed commercial terms and identifies exact reservations/shipments, indicative allocated supply and potential shared work/procurement with incurred material-cost sources and remaining exposure. An authorized sales/manager person approves the exact preview; any intervening business write requires a new review. The screen uses this same reviewed amendment for cancellation; the existing direct cancellation API also requires the customer attestation and retains before/after history. Every line retains its stable identity, cumulative ordered/cancelled quantities and shipped/returned balances. Edited quantity means the non-cancelled total including shipped units; dated delivery quantities sum only the outstanding balance. Shipping consumes earliest outstanding dates; direct cancellation removes latest dates first. The compatible order promise date is the latest remaining delivery date, while planning retains the individual dated pegs; when no delivery remains, the last approved promise date stays as history. Customer approval dates are real calendar dates no later than today in UTC. Price changes apply prospectively to unshipped quantities; picked shipment prices/addresses remain immutable. Picked/packed quantities require an explicit undo-pick before cancellation, and excess reservations are released without consuming any location’s picked commitment. Work and supplier commitments remain visible and require their own authorized change. Historical shipments missing an issued price are labelled incomplete; repricing, invoice values, returns or corrections requiring that missing price are refused pending source-document reconciliation, with no current-price substitution.

## DEMAND-003

Planners maintain time-bucketed sales forecasts and a master production schedule, import forecast CSV, distinguish actual orders from forecast consumption, and record overrides, seasonality and forecast error. Actual demand consumes forecasts rather than doubling them. Weeks start Monday at 00:00 UTC with an exclusive next-Monday end; quantities use each SKU's base unit. Baseline × nonnegative seasonality rounds half away from zero to six places, and an explicit nonnegative override replaces that result. Confirmed outstanding schedules and issued shipped-delivery quantities consume forecast for the same SKU/week; quotes and cancelled quantities do not. Remaining commercial demand is outstanding orders plus max, with existing hard reservations kept separate. Error is actual ordered demand minus adjusted forecast, including fulfilled deliveries, rather than a claim of completed-period accuracy. Historical shipped quantities without issued delivery-date evidence are explicit and block MPS preparation pending source reconciliation. The MPS pools free available stock and existing draft/open supply by week (receipt on or before Sunday); suggestions cover only the remaining commercial quantity and carry unused common stock forward. This weekly quantity plan does not claim finite capacity or change customer promises. An authorized human releases the exact state-version preview to linked draft work/purchases; work release and purchase spending approval remain their existing separate doors. Manager/buyer/production/planner/agent prepare imports and MPS previews, manager/production/planner record reasoned seasonality/override changes and undo, and owner/manager/production/planner approve release (a different preparer except the owner); An agent’s equivalent call carries its computed risk classes and the company’s policy decides it; a person’s signature is recorded only after that person acts. Every original CSV/digest and before/after change remains retained, and undo appends the prior basis with its reason instead of deleting history. The GOV-001 planner role carries this forecast/MPS authority and separation. The location policy planner is the default for configured SKU/location sizing. The existing global DEMAND-003 MPS remains an explicit manual exception: its preview shows the effective policies and current catalog settings for suggested supply; release requires a human acknowledgment and recorded reason, retains the exact weekly quantities without automatic location sizing, and pins that policy evidence and decision on the plan and linked drafts. Existing independent human release authority still applies and An agent’s equivalent call carries its computed risk classes and the company’s policy decides it; a person’s signature is recorded only after that person acts.

Existing hard reservations remain protected across all bins. The protected floor is max(safety stock, minimum stock); common available stock above it is shared only through exact reviewed allocations. For make-to-stock, projected inventory nets selected outstanding demand and located open/draft supply; at or below reorder it replenishes toward max(maximum stock, floor), otherwise only a floor shortfall is suggested. Make-to-order and assemble-to-order retain separate customer/order-line/date pegs for uncovered demand; assembly previews resolve the effective BOM and show shared component availability, shortage and late supply. Batch size and order multiple are base quantities; suggestions round upward to their least common exact six-place multiple. Located dedicated future supply stays with its exact order/line, and late supply nets quantity without claiming on-time coverage. Unlocated open supply blocks approval until a manager/production person or planner records its exact-version bin and optional customer peg without changing quantity, price or date. Existing assigned commitments cannot be silently relocated. A manager/buyer/production person, planner or agent prepares; owner/manager/production/planner approves an exact state-version preview, independently except the owner. Approval records planning-bin metadata, reserves only the displayed common available quantities and creates linked draft work or purchases; physical receipt/output, work release and spending retain their separate authorized doors. Earlier policy/preview/approval and demand pegs remain in the complete archive; current catalog edits invalidate old previews rather than rewriting them. The GOV-001 planner role carries this maintenance and independent approval authority. The location policy planner is the default for configured SKU/location sizing. The existing global DEMAND-003 MPS remains an explicit manual exception: its preview shows the effective policies and current catalog settings for suggested supply; release requires a human acknowledgment and recorded reason, retains the exact weekly quantities without automatic location sizing, and pins that policy evidence and decision on the plan and linked drafts. Existing independent human release authority still applies and An agent’s equivalent call carries its computed risk classes and the company’s policy decides it; a person’s signature is recorded only after that person acts.

## DEMAND-007

Accounts invoice a sales order from the order or the SO list with *Invoice all* (every packed or delivered line) or *Invoice some…* (chosen packed or delivered quantities only), for one order or in bulk across selected orders, each producing exactly one invoice per order with stable source IDs, the order's prices, discounts, freight and tax, and the invoice's lines linked to their shipments. An optional rule invoices orders automatically when they are packed, shipped or delivered and can send the invoice to the customer by BUY-007. Quantity not yet packed or delivered, or already invoiced, cannot be invoiced; a reversed shipment produces a linked credit. The SO list and the state API show every order's invoice status (Not invoiced, Partially invoiced, Invoiced) and payment status (Unpaid, Partially paid, Paid, with amount outstanding and due date), the payment status read back from the connected books (CONN-001 QuickBooks Online, CONN-002 Xero), which stay the payment authority. Both columns sort and filter, and a configurable release check warns or blocks shipping a wholesale order whose earlier invoices are overdue. Invoice commands are idempotent.

## MRP-001

MRP nets dated sales, forecast, dependent BOM demand and safety stock against usable inventory, reservations, yields, open POs, WIP and lead times. Late, held or expiring supply cannot cover earlier demand. Each suggestion explains quantity, date and sources. The current slice is the read-only dated physical/purchase/work projection at `/api/larder/planning/materials`: direct outstanding sales, residual forecasts and pinned released-work inputs share physical lots/bins and dated outstanding POs once. Hard bin reservations are protected first; lots are judged on the later of the required UTC day and today, with inclusive expiry/retest and physical customer shelf-life rules. Unusable reservations stay protected and explicit, never silently reassigned. Late/overdue unreceived POs, quarantine, draft POs and unproven work output cannot masquerade as on-time usable supply. After hard reservations, current physical safety floors protect the greater of catalog safetyStock and minStock at each active SKU’s default bin (or its catalog location when no default is set). Catalog locations naming the same SKU/bin contribute their maximum floor once, not a sum. Floor rows precede free-demand allocation, use only lots eligible today in that bin, and show any uncovered quantity explicitly; unreceived POs cannot establish a current physical floor. This is a read-only planning protection, not a new physical reservation or dispatch gate. Future dated floor replenishment: once every dated demand has taken its supply, what remains of approved open purchases and proven work output restores an uncovered floor in date order. Each floor row lists those dated receipts and the day it is restored; that supply never counts as physical cover, and the buy suggestion covers only the part nothing has scheduled. Informational bought-item suggestions subtract recorded supplier lead days in UTC calendar days, keep missing lead time unknown and flag past releases; this does not approve or create supply. When the preferred supplier has structured terms, the suggestion follows its calendar instead: the receipt day is the latest open supplier day on or before the required day, the order day is the latest day whose lead working days (closed dates and non-working weekdays do not count) arrive by then, and the quantity rounds up to the supplier's minimum order and pack, with the shortage and quoted unit price shown beside it. The dated projection now explodes uncovered make and phantom demand through today’s effective multilevel BOMs, preserving ENG-001 make/buy/phantom choices and aggregate upward six-place yield rounding. Every dependent row retains its root document/line, required UTC day and ordered BOM/revision path; shared physical and dated PO pools are netted once per item after all its parent branches. Customer shelf-life applies to finished sales stock, while dependent materials use internal lot eligibility. Recorded manufacturing routings now provide informational full-batch lead offsets: each operation takes setup + queue + run minutes times uncovered good-output base units, starts after all its declared predecessors (legacy order implies the previous operation), and the longest finishing path is rounded upward once to whole UTC calendar days. Minute arithmetic uses the stored decimal representation exactly; independent branches share elapsed time. Transfer-batch overlap, work-center/labor calendars and capacity contention are not applied, so this is not a feasible schedule. Components are due at the parent’s calculated release day; make levels accumulate offsets, while a phantom adds no separate manufacturing operation. Each row/path exposes its timing basis, BOM, receipt/release days and past-release warning; a make shortage with a representable recorded release gets an informational make suggestion. Missing or unrepresentable routing release dates provide timing diagnostics without a make suggestion. A missing/empty routing keeps lead and release unknown and explicitly labels inherited component dates as unoffset; a recorded zero-duration routing is zero days. Released inputs use the full pinned job quantity/routing and declared expectedOn completion day, falling back to requiredBy, less already-issued component quantities; later BOM edits and partial output never invent remaining operation time. A duration outside the existing four-digit YYYY-MM-DD calendar stays explicitly blocked with unknown release, never crashes the projection or supplies a plausible dated recipe. None of these reads changes promises, reservations, drafts or stock. Outstanding work is material-feasible when it is released and every pinned input still to be issued is covered, after the shared allocation, by usable stock or dated supply, including the proven output of other jobs ; its completion day must not have passed and its routing must stay inside the calendar. A material-feasible job’s remaining output nets demand on its completion day like an approved purchase, sources show it by job, and only the uncovered remainder explodes further. Draft work pins no inputs and any unproven job keeps the item’s whole remainder in blockedExplosions, so a sales recipe is never counted again beside existing work; workFeasibility lists every outstanding job with its completion day, remaining output, shortages and reason, and released pinned inputs remain separate demand. An authorized `plan.draft` preparer can now select one exact dated suggestion with its projection version, UTC day and requirement key. Preparation revalidates all current inputs and the day atomically, derives the shortage quantity, supplier and receipt/release dates on the server, and retains the reviewed root/revision path and timing in the ordinary draft. A purchase draft requires an explicitly entered unit price. Existing inventory-policy routing and purchase/work approval authorities still apply; preparation never approves supply, reserves stock, changes promises or claims a feasible schedule. Open supply already linked to the same root/component branch prevents another dated draft, including after date or BOM revision edits; a repeated command replays its first result. Blocked explosions and missing suggestions cannot be drafted through this door. Reads disclose linked supply without counting unapproved drafts as usable coverage. MOVE-008 export/restore retains draft provenance and duplicate guards without executing work. The projection’s remaining boundary is unchanged: it is informational and never a feasible finite schedule, an approval or a promise change. The projection introduces no persisted records; MOVE-008 exports preserve its inputs and restore must reproduce its dated result without executing any plan.

## MRP-002

A planner follows any component, receipt or work order to the customer demand it supplies and reverse-pegs all affected orders. Canceled/late/excess supply produces expedite, defer or cancel recommendations with impact and approval. The current partial read-only door `/api/larder/planning/pegging` follows an item, sales order, forecast, purchase order/line, receipt/line or lot through the shared dated MRP-001 allocation, retaining root demand, component/revision paths, exact base-unit quantities, source dates and protected-reservation flags. Purchase lookup includes both outstanding lines and usable stock from its recorded receipt lots. Late purchase candidates are unallocated alternatives for shortages, never quantities to sum or promises of coverage. Received stock can be reverse-pegged only through recorded lots; unlotted receipts remain explicit untracked lines, without inferred ancestry. Work lookup exposes pinned component inputs and outstanding-work blockers, without inventing customer links through provisional output. Reads preserve physical commitments, create no records or outbound work, and reproduce from the MOVE-008 business archive after restore. Output of a material-feasible released job (MRP-001) is a work source on the demand it supplies, so work lookup follows it to that customer demand. *Supply changes a read of open approved purchase lines recommends an expedite where a line arrives after short requirements (to the earliest such day, for the quantity it could cover), a cancel for outstanding quantity no demand or floor allocates, and a deferral where everything it supplies is first needed seven or more days after it arrives, each naming its affected orders and exact quantities; it is recomputed on every read. A buyer or manager approves or rejects one with a reason against the current version and UTC day (a cancel only the manager or owner): an approved expedite or deferral is a recorded request that changes no supplier date, stock or reservation and sends nothing, an approved cancel trims only the unallocated outstanding quantity and its exposure, and every decision keeps an approval record and audit entry. the owner decides as well; an approval records the exact recommendation inputs it was given for (the recommendation, the state version and the UTC day it was read on, with a digest) and is refused when they changed; a cancel whose value (cancelled quantity at the line price) is at or above the owner's spend threshold needs the GOV-002 signatures on the exact cancellation. MRP-002 counts for the decision record only: updating the PO line's promise and reaching the supplier is MRP-005, carved from this clause.

## CAP-002

Supervisors schedule employees/teams by shift, availability, time off, skills and simultaneous-task limits, independent of machine availability. Wage/burden rates are restricted to accounts/authorized managers; time capture feeds costing rather than payroll. Payroll sibling: [Envelope](https://github.com/just-every/run-envelope). Person/team calendars name active native people and supervisor-authorized skill names, with independent timezone/shift/time-off availability and a task limit per named person; one person belongs to one calendar. ENG-002 supplies owner/manager supervision authority; GOV-001 supplies owner/manager/accounts employment-rate authority. Existing exact task assignments constrain projected people but a projection grants no execution assignment or certification. Employment wage/burden records and their history are omitted from other roles’ state and plan reads; operators cannot recover rework hourly-rate fields through a prior command replay, while FLOOR-004 retains its explicit production authoring authority. Completed operation clocks minus recorded pauses feed restricted costing evidence at the retained USD rate in force at start, with six-place half-up rounding and unknown cost when no historical rate exists; no payroll or ledger posting occurs. FLOOR-002 and COST-001 inherit corrections/period locks and posted actual-cost duties. Calendars, skills, rates/history and clock evidence inherit MOVE-008 JSON/CSV archive and empty restore; unmapped native people remain inert, asserted in the named journey.

## CAP-003

Operations obey predecessor completion, transfer batches, overlap rules, material availability and quality gates. A blocked task shows its exact reason and owner, and becomes dispatchable only after the dependency is satisfied. Before any new operation material/quality gate ships, expose its exact live blocked reason in FLOOR-001 dispatch and enforce the same condition at operation start. ** Immutable routing revisions pin each operation’s predecessors, `transferBatch` in finished-item base units, `allowOverlap` and `requireMaterials`. A zero batch (the default) uses full predecessor completion without a separate handoff gate. A positive batch requires cumulative actual handoffs from every direct predecessor of at least min(batch, job quantity). Overlap is off by default and requires a positive batch when enabled; without overlap each predecessor must also be complete. Handoffs record additional completed units physically transferred, the assigned starter, server time and reason, never FLOOR-007 progress estimates or finished stock. They cannot exceed the job quantity or, for a batch-gated source, the quantity actually received from every predecessor. A batch-gated operation cannot complete before it has received the full job quantity.
