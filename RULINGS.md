### RL-9 — Purchasing approvals and three-way match

- Date: 2026-10-26
- Ruler: ct (as recorded in the carried contract)

### RL-101 — Exact bounded cost-analysis disclosure

- Date: 2026-10-28
- Ruler: ct
- Governs: AGENT-003
- Outcome: **adopt with rider** — Permit one explicitly consented minimized snapshot to the exact declared processor route under the selected token/USD ceilings and disclosed retention terms; source or destination changes require fresh review, and output can only propose future human approved revisions.

**Question:** May the native owner authorize one minimized cost snapshot to the declared model destination with capped spend and retained hypotheses?

**Adopted/proposed default:** Owner-only prepare/consent/send/revoke/follow-through; exact snapshot and selected direct Anthropic or routed OpenRouter destination; no fallback; explicit retention acknowledgment; capped tokens/USD; no automatic effects.

**Incumbent behavior and why:** Katana requires an explicitly enabled MCP connection, but its current MCP authority spans account-wide read/create/update rather than exact one-shot cost analysis. No common norm establishes owner-only analysis, a specific model, tiny cap or transient consent. Prefer the narrower exact-disclosure policy because output is a hypothesis and business/financial revisions remain independently approved.

**Single placement:** AGENT-003 owns the disclosure/cap rule once; AGENT-006/009 cite it and existing engineering/accounting approval clauses retain revision authority.

**Engineering/proof boundary:** A direct-provider workspace-header failure is an integration configuration defect, not a pending product-policy decision; a separately selected routed success does not qualify the failed direct path. No model output posts money or changes historical actuals.

### RL-102 — Attributed time corrections and costing periods

- Date: 2026-10-28
- Ruler: ct
- Governs: FLOOR-002
- Outcome: **adopt with rider** — Adopt accounts/owner period control, attributed reasoned independent manager correction with the disclosed owner exception, nonoverlapping bounded operational entries and retained start-time rates; a fallback rate must be identified as a costing assumption when historical rate authority is absent, and a refused long span must remain recoverable as actual worked segments without silent truncation or payroll posting.

**Question:** May accounts/owner lock periods and independent managers correct bounded time using retained effective rates without payroll posting?

**Adopted/proposed default:** Accounts/owner lock and reopen; different manager decides corrections except owner; maximum sixteen-hour entry; effective start rate with first-clock fallback; no payroll posting.

**Incumbent behavior and why:** Katana records actual operation time and permits main-site edits; MRPeasy uses permissioned user wages and working-day timers. No common norm establishes sixteen hours, owner self-correction or the precise rate fallback. Keep the conservative operational limits, with honest unresolved-rate treatment and complete actual time preserved; none is a wage-law or payroll-compliance rule.

**Single placement:** FLOOR-002 owns correction/period/rate semantics; cite GOV-001 for existing wage/location restrictions rather than duplicating them.

**Engineering/proof boundary:** Preserve the 21 October GOV-001 scope and restricted-wage riders. The sixteen-hour entry bound is not a daily work or pay limit; accounting approval cannot fabricate a missing historical effective rate. Wider payroll remains outside this time-costing door.

**Sources:**
