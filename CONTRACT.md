# Larder product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Larder is the manufacturing ERP/MRP a US small discrete manufacturer or maker can switch to from Katana, MRPeasy, Fishbowl Manufacturing, Odoo Manufacturing, Fulcrum or DOSS. It turns demand into a feasible material and capacity plan, executes shop-floor work, traces lots and quality, costs actual production, ships orders and reconciles connected books.

This is the product we are building toward. A missing capability is engineering work, not a reduced promise. Each named clause states buyer behavior and acceptance evidence through a public screen, API, command, download or provider connection. The final Status table measures current code against the whole destination; [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) lists the same journeys and statuses. Named acceptance journeys for missing work are requirements, not claims that tests already exist.

## Product target

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Manufacturing ERP / MRP for small manufacturers and makers |
| Incumbents a buyer switches from | Katana, MRPeasy, Fishbowl Manufacturing, Odoo Manufacturing, Fulcrum, DOSS |
| First buyer | A US owner or operations manager of a 5–50-person discrete manufacturer/maker, selling made goods in USD, with shop-floor, warehouse, purchasing and accounts staff and QuickBooks Online or Xero as the authoritative external ledger |

The whole job includes engineering records, quotes and orders, make-to-stock/order, multilevel MRP, finite machine/labor scheduling, purchasing/subcontracting, tablet/barcode execution, stock and genealogy, food/cosmetic/supplement batch expiry and recall records, actual job/WIP costs, reporting, commerce and shipping, role controls, migration and recovery. Agents maintain approved plans and follow consequences through actual production and books. Operational quality/traceability is part of the product; qualified people remain responsible for release and regulatory decisions.

The sixteen published clauses keep their IDs and useful operational guarantees. The accountant CSV handoff stays a compatibility/export flow inside connected accounting; it is no longer the destination. Historical Australian/AUD defaults (superseded by the 17 October 2026 target edition) and prior exclusions of capacity, integrations, barcode, serials, native apps and model calls are superseded by the target clauses. New US settings require an explicit migration of existing currency/identity data, never relabeling AUD amounts as USD.

## Operating rules

- Start empty. No sample stock, customers, synthetic provider outcomes or fallback balances enter a live business.
- Stock truth is append-only signed movements by item, location, status and lot/serial. Availability separates on-hand, reservations, quarantine, transit and expected supply. Database guards prevent negative positions and overselling regardless of caller.
- Exact fixed-point quantities/money use up to six decimal places; document/ledger rounding rules are explicit and versioned. Conversions must be defined and exact. Used units are immutable; changing their meaning needs a new SKU.
- Saved BOM revisions are immutable; released work pins BOM/routing/instruction/cost assumptions. Corrections are linked compensating movements; downstream dependencies block reversal until resolved.
- Every write is atomic and rechecks live native session, current role and state version. Stable command identities replay recorded outcomes; changed content under an identity is refused. New adapters use these same doors and invariants.
- Hard reservations do not silently expire. Reallocation, changed promises, purchasing, financial posting and quality disposition require the right recorded authority.
- Agents prepare; authorized people approve exact versioned inputs and external consequences. Untrusted inquiries, supplier files and drawings are evidence, never instructions. Deterministic arithmetic and ledger/physical evidence decide quantities and amounts.
- Provider app registration, OAuth review, carrier/marketplace access, model configuration and regulated deployment agreements are delivery work. Each connection must prove permitted provider readback; local test doubles at paid/network edges do not establish live acceptance.


## Published operational foundation

- **LARDER-001 — Items, variants, units and parties.** A person creates items (made or bought), variants of a template with distinct attribute sets, exact unit conversions, customers and suppliers, and each party's own code for an item. Ambiguous conversions, duplicate SKUs or variants, ambiguous party codes and duplicate party names are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Through authenticated HTTP create two variants, exact unit conversions and supplier/customer codes; refuse duplicate SKUs, variants, ambiguous codes, undefined or rounding conversions, and edits to used units.

- **LARDER-002 — Opening stock.** A person records a counted opening position by location with its count date, valuation basis (supplier invoices, last purchase, standard cost or owner estimate), unit cost, optional lot and a required evidence file kept with its digest. Opening stock cannot be posted twice for an item and location. **Acceptance evidence:** Record a counted, costed opening with its evidence attachment; download that attachment, reconcile the position, and refuse a second opening for the same item/location.

- **LARDER-003 — Versioned BOM and routing.** A person saves BOM revisions (components per finished unit, simple routing steps). Releasing work pins the active revision; a later revision does not change released work. **Acceptance evidence:** Release work on revision 1, save revision 2 and release another job; the first job keeps revision 1 and its original components/routing.

- **LARDER-004 — Quote and confirm.** A person quotes an order and confirms it. For every item Larder shows on hand, reserved, quarantine, expected from purchase, expected from production, unreserved demand and available to promise, each kept separate. **Acceptance evidence:** Quote and confirm an order through the order screen/API; inspect on-hand, reserved, quarantine, expected purchase/production and unreserved demand as separate figures.

- **LARDER-005 — Atomic reservation.** Confirming or reserving takes only available stock. Concurrent orders cannot both consume the same stock; a retried command returns its first outcome. **Acceptance evidence:** Race six HTTP reservations for the same stock; total reservations never exceed stock, an identical command replays its first outcome and changed content under its identity is refused.

- **LARDER-006 — Shortages.** Larder computes shortages deterministically from confirmed orders and released work against free stock and open supply, with the date needed and the documents that need it, and drafts a purchase order (preferred supplier and price) or a work order with that required-by date and reason. **Acceptance evidence:** Confirm an order exceeding free stock, draft its work shortage, release work, and draft the resulting purchased-component shortage; quantities, required dates and source documents agree.

- **LARDER-007 — Purchase approval.** A buyer or manager approves a draft purchase order (approval recorded with the exact terms and cash exposure), records the supplier's confirmation and reference, and revises the expected delivery with history. **Acceptance evidence:** Draft and approve a PO, confirm supplier reference/date, then revise its ETA; read exact approved terms, cash exposure and every date revision.

- **LARDER-008 — Partial receipt, lots and quarantine.** A warehouse person receives part of a purchase order into a location, records supplier lots and either accepts or holds stock in quarantine; a recorded release or rejection decision moves it on. Over-receipt is refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Part-receive a PO with supplier lot into quarantine; release part and reject part with reasons; refuse over-receipt and prevent held stock from reservation/picking.

- **LARDER-009 — Production.** Released work reserves its components; materials are issued as actually used (drawn across lots when no lot is named), good output and scrap are recorded against the pinned revision, and closing records material cost and variance. **Acceptance evidence:** Release work, issue actual components across lots, record good output and scrap, and close; inspect pinned BOM, cost and material variance through the API and work-order screen.

- **LARDER-010 — Pick, pack and partial shipment.** A warehouse person picks only reserved stock, packs, and ships part of an order; shipping consumes those reservations, and the remaining commitments stay reserved or short. **Acceptance evidence:** Reserve an order, pick only reserved stock, pack and partly ship; read remaining reservations/shortages and refuse an unreserved pick through HTTP. Pack a shipment with its carton count, ship it, and read back that count, actual departure timestamp, carrier and tracking against the sales order through the API.

- **LARDER-011 — Returns, transfers, counts and corrections.** Returns of shipped quantities come back to stock or quarantine; transfers move only free stock; counts by one person need another's approval before an adjustment (the owner, who holds every authority, may approve their own count so a one-person workshop can keep its stock right); a stale count is refused; reversals restore stock and order state through compensating movements and are refused while downstream work depends on the stock. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive a return, transfer free stock and approve another person's count; reject stale counts and downstream-dependent reversals; reverse shipment by compensating movements and retain its original.

- **LARDER-012 — Accountant handoff.** A manager or accounts person prepares an immutable batch of not-yet-handed-off movements, valued: supplier receipts, customer shipments for invoice preparation (with the order price), production issue and output, adjustments, returns and corrections. The batch downloads as CSV. Acknowledgments record ledger references line by line; differing amounts show as mismatches until corrected; a later reversal appears in the next batch as a correction pointing to the original batch and line. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare/download valued handoff CSV, acknowledge ledger references with a mismatch, correct it, reverse an earlier shipment and see its linked correction in the next batch.

- **LARDER-013 — Exceptions and the complete archive.** One queue shows shortages, overdue purchases, quarantine, waiting approvals and proposals, unreconciled handoffs and rejected move-in rows; a manager records decision and follow-up notes. Under AUTO-001 a note remains in history and the case stays visible as following up while its source condition holds; only clearing that condition closes it. The owner or a manager exports the complete business archive with a checksum; the owner restores it into an empty installation in one atomic write that proves every stock position against the archive. **Acceptance evidence:** Record a decision follow-up note, verify the ongoing exception stays visible and the note survives restore; export/archive and restore into an empty installation; refuse a damaged archive and prove every restored position; round-trip an installation backup after migration/reset.

- **LARDER-014 — Authority everywhere.** Every command and read checks the current session and role in the handler and again inside the atomic write; losing a role or signing out takes effect on the next request; cross-site commands are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Call every protected door as signed out, viewer and restricted roles; revoke a role and sign out, then reuse the old cookie; cross-site commands and writes with stale live authority leave no mutation.

- **LARDER-015 — Agents prepare, people approve.** Through the same public command API an agent prepares: order intake from a pasted inquiry (kept as data, never obeyed; missing quantities block approval), replenishment as grouped purchase orders over deterministic shortages with its explanation and cash exposure, a duplicate-item merge with its previewed impact (stock moved by compensating movements), a work-order reschedule with conflicts, and a ledger reconciliation. A person with the right role, never the preparer (except the owner, who holds every authority) and never an agent, approves or declines the exact version; stale inputs are refused; every decision is recorded. **Acceptance evidence:** As an agent prepare intake, replenishment, merge, reschedule and reconciliation proposals; refuse incomplete/stale inputs and agent/self approval, then approve/decline exact versions as authorized people and inspect decisions.

- **LARDER-016 — Odoo kit.** A manager (or an agent, for preparation) previews Odoo import-compatible CSV exports of contacts, units, products and variants, locations, BOMs with routing, on-hand quantities and open sales and purchase orders. The preview is a dry run listing counts, rejected rows with file, line, External ID and reason, deliberate skips and unread columns. Committing publishes through Base's import kit, which journals the job and keeps every original file and digest. Undo before live use removes the whole batch; once later work depends on it, undo is refused and points at the dependency. The documented shape is in [MOVE-IN.md](MOVE-IN.md). **Acceptance evidence:** Upload Odoo import-compatible CSV via move-in, inspect file/line/External-ID errors, skips/unread columns, commit, inspect retained originals/digests and undo; malformed/replayed inputs and undo after live dependencies are refused.


## Target clauses

### US operating setup

- **US-001 — US business and USD defaults.** A US manufacturer sets legal name, US billing/shipping addresses, EIN reference, USD functional currency, US time zone, working week and fiscal year. Quantities support exact US customary and metric units; customer/supplier tax identities are distinct from Australian ABNs. Money and archives retain their declared currency. **Acceptance evidence:** Set up an Ohio workshop in USD, quote in inches and receive in feet with an exact conversion; export and restore the order and verify local dates, currency and identity. Reject an inexact conversion. Each archived monetary amount retains its ISO 4217 currency, either on the amount or through an unambiguous currency declaration for its record; USD is the default for a new US installation. Restore preserves original currencies. Existing AUD data requires an explicit versioned migration with recorded rates and reconciliation, never a USD relabel. Compare a new USD archive and a migrated legacy AUD archive after restore, including all amount currencies and migration totals.

- **US-002 — Sales tax and exemption handoff.** Manufacturing quotes/orders carry ship-to jurisdiction, taxable items, freight, exemption/resale certificates and provider-calculated US sales tax via Avalara AvaTax or TaxJar. Final invoice tax goes to the connected ledger once; expiry or provider failure blocks an unsupported tax assertion. Larder retains the calculation and reversal references. **Acceptance evidence:** Quote an interstate taxable order and a resale-exempt order through the UI with a tax sandbox; ship/return part and reconcile invoice and tax reversal in QuickBooks Online.

- **US-003 — Guided real-order setup.** Setup guides the owner through units, locations, suppliers, BOMs, calendars, costing and a real order to shipment, showing missing prerequisites and understandable migration diagnostics. It starts empty, and progress resumes without a consulting-only configuration path. **Acceptance evidence:** In an empty installation complete setup using an actual workshop order; interrupt after BOM setup, resume and reach a feasible plan, production, shipment and reconciled ledger entry.


### Product engineering

- **ENG-001 — Multilevel BOM explosion.** Engineers build multilevel assemblies and phantom subassemblies with per-unit quantities, yields, effective revisions and make/buy choices. MRP recursively explodes them, detects cycles, and pegs component demand without double counting shared subassemblies. Released work remains pinned. Yield is a good-output fraction greater than zero and at most one; each aggregate recipe requirement divides per-unit inputs by yield and rounds upward to six quantity places. Planning and release select the revision effective today in UTC. Make supply nets shared stock/open and draft supply before exploding only uncovered quantity; buy stops explosion; phantom bypasses subassembly stock and expands its recipe. Draft work contributes its inputs once; released work uses its pinned requirements. Pegs retain the root document and revision path. Future effective revisions are also checked for cycles. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a three-level BOM sharing a component, stock one subassembly, run MRP and inspect net component demand; reject a cycle and preserve released work after revision.

- **ENG-002 — Executable routings and instructions.** A revision includes ordered operations, dependencies, setup/run/queue times, work-center eligibility, labor skills, drawings and step instructions. Operators see the exact approved revision at their task, including a required acknowledgment after an instruction change. Setup and queue estimates are minutes per job; run time is minutes per base-unit output (legacy `minutes` aliases run time), each finite in the inclusive range 0–100000. Dependencies name unique earlier operation sequence numbers, defaulting to the previous operation; an explicit empty list permits an independent step. Eligible center names match exactly. The production role and the GOV-001 engineer author routings, and production people and GOV-001 operators operate them; the owner or manager records a named active production person’s or operator’s skill authorization for the exact task before starting work that requires those skills. This is a human authorization, never certification. The assigned person and current native role are checked at execution; agents cannot authorize, acknowledge, start or complete tasks. Saving through the authorized engineering door records the authorizing person and time. Release snapshots instructions and original drawing files/digests, and each job starts with unacknowledged tasks. A person acknowledges that job’s exact pinned BOM before starting an instruction/drawing task; predecessor completion and eligible center selection are enforced through both tablet and command API. Future revision/intake doors in ENG-004, ENG-005, CONN-011, MOVE-001 through MOVE-006 and AGENT-005 inherit the same pinning and acknowledgment obligations before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Release a two-operation job, open its tablet task and drawing, then revise instructions; the released job retains its instructions and a new job requires acknowledgment.

- **ENG-003 — Configurable and matrix BOMs.** Sales/engineering choose permitted variant attributes, dimensions and options; rules derive component quantities, routing and cost with explainable formulas. Invalid combinations are refused and the resolved configuration is pinned on quote and work. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure two sizes/finishes through the quote form, compare resolved BOM/cost and release both; refuse an invalid combination and keep old work stable after a rule edit.

- **ENG-004 — BOM CSV export and bulk revision.** Engineers export BOMs/routings as CSV and preview batch component substitutions, quantity changes and effective dates with affected SKUs, stock and open-work impact. Approval creates revisions atomically; released work changes only by a separately reviewed change. **Acceptance evidence:** Export ten BOMs, replace one component through CSV preview, inspect row errors and affected work, approve and re-export; a released job keeps its previous revision.

- **ENG-005 — Engineering change approval and obsolescence.** Engineers propose changes with reason, effectivity, attachments, substitute eligibility and disposition of obsolete stock; authorized managers approve, record acknowledgment and choose treatment of unreleased work. Approved revision history is immutable. Production, a GOV-001 engineer or a manager prepares, and owner/manager approves an exact state version, with a different approver except the owner; engineers never approve. A pending safety change blocks new affected work releases; an open proposal also blocks direct and CSV BOM revision bypass. Each affected draft, including a parent that flattens a changed phantom, receives a reasoned keep-previous, adopt-approved or cancel decision. Adoption cannot release before the approved UTC effective date; keeping uses the explicitly reviewed previous revision. The releasing production person acknowledges the exact approved change before affected work releases; task instructions/drawings still require ENG-002’s job-level acknowledgment. Substitutes name the removed and replacement components with eligibility evidence. Every positive removed-component stock position has a quantity/location/lot/disposition instruction and reason; these instructions never claim a physical return, rework or scrap movement. Actual stock actions use their own authorized doors. Evidence files and drawing bytes/digests, source/prospective/approved revisions, decisions and acknowledgments remain in the archive. An intervening business write refuses approval; decline and propose a new review. **Acceptance evidence:** Propose a safety-related component replacement with an attachment and substitute eligibility, deny direct and phantom-parent release before approval and refuse BOM bypass, independently approve on an effective date, acknowledge it and inspect old stock instructions plus keep/adopt/cancel draft outcomes and unchanged released jobs. Refuse premature adoption, stale/self/agent approval; restore the evidence and immutable review history, and require the new job’s pinned instruction acknowledgment.

- **ENG-006 — Kits and sale-triggered assembly.** Sellable kits have versioned component definitions; an authorized sale can trigger policy-approved assembly or component picking. Stock, kit availability and costs reconcile once even when the channel repeats the sale event. **Acceptance evidence:** Sell a kit twice via one replayed channel event and a separate second sale; verify two assemblies only, exact component consumption and no oversell.

- **ENG-007 — Catalog export, bulk edit and re-import.** An authorized manager, buyer or engineer downloads the active catalog of items and variants as XLSX or CSV, with the Larder ID and every editable field per row (per location where the field is location-specific): SKU, name, category, sales price and price list, purchase price, default supplier and supplier code, barcode/GTIN, reorder point, safety stock, min/max, default bin, batch/serial tracking, lead time and active/archived state. Re-uploading the edited file matches rows by Larder ID; rows without an ID are creates. An empty cell leaves the stored value unchanged and only an explicit clear marker removes a value. The preview lists each changed field (old and new value), each row error (file, row, column, reason) and what each change affects: open quotes and orders, planning policies, open POs and tracked stock. Approval applies all accepted rows atomically against the previewed state version. Used units, stock movements, reservations and issued documents are never rewritten, and turning on batch/serial tracking for an item with untracked stock is refused with its reason. CSV is the delivered format; the download includes every current editable item field and one row per active location, with locations serving as bins. Global edits on repeated location rows must agree, and an invalid row excludes its whole item group. Blank cells preserve values; #CLEAR removes an optional value, with a leading backslash escaping a literal #CLEAR (one added backslash escapes a preceding backslash run). Text exports carry one reversible leading apostrophe, preserving numeric identifiers, leading zeros and formula-like text. A new item's ID-less location rows group by SKU. Supplier terms retain their purchase unit in party/item references; catalog edits affect future documents and never reprice saved ones. Location reorder/safety/min/max/bin/lead settings are retained configuration for DEMAND-004; that clause must honor them before its policy planner ships. Manager/buyer/production and the GOV-001 engineer role export, prepare and approve catalog edits, with a different human approver except the owner. Agents cannot approve or enable tracking. Tracking selection is a declared catalog policy: batch requires a valid lot on current physical receipts/output, while serial is labelled “serial policy configured; serial execution not yet available” and current physical receipt/output is refused. Enabling batch is refused for any nonzero unlotted position across all locations/dispositions; serial selection is refused while any nonzero physical stock exists. TRACE-002 owns serial-aware execution and must honor the configured serial policy before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Export items and variants for two locations, change sales prices, reorder points and default suppliers on several rows, leave other cells blank, and add one bad row (unknown supplier) and one change to a used unit. The preview lists both row errors and each row's impact. Approve, re-export and compare: only the edited fields changed, blank cells kept existing data, used units and stock history are unchanged, and a preview made stale by a concurrent edit is refused.


### Demand and sales

- **DEMAND-001 — Costed quotes and credible promise dates.** Sales prepares versioned quotes in USD, or in a customer's own currency such as CAD with the recorded rate and USD functional value, including customer-specific prices, quantity breaks, discounts, freight, quote validity and estimated material/labor/overhead margin. Available-to-promise and capable-to-promise dates use dated supply and finite material/labor capacity, exposing assumptions and alternatives. **Acceptance evidence:** Quote a job with scarce material and a busy machine, compare immediate/partial/overtime options and their margin/date, accept one and trace the date to booked supply/capacity; quote a Canadian wholesale customer in CAD and read the order, invoice and margin in CAD with their USD valuation at the recorded rate. Show three feasible ship-date alternatives for an order, each checked against the material, machine and qualified-person calendars before it is offered. Reconcile quoted line extensions and the order total to the cent using the documented fixed-point rounding rules, including a fractional quantity and price.

- **DEMAND-002 — Order changes and cancellations.** Sales versions order quantities, dates, addresses and prices with customer approval history. Changes identify affected reservations, procurement, work and shipped balances; cancellation releases only unconsumed commitments and leaves already incurred costs visible. Customer approval is the authorized salesperson’s locally recorded attestation (name, source reference, received date and optional original evidence), never a claim of remote identity validation. An amendment preview retains old/proposed commercial terms and identifies exact reservations/shipments, indicative allocated supply and potential shared work/procurement with incurred material-cost sources and remaining exposure. An authorized sales/manager person approves the exact preview; any intervening business write requires a new review. The screen uses this same reviewed amendment for cancellation; the existing direct cancellation API also requires the customer attestation and retains before/after history. Every line retains its stable identity, cumulative ordered/cancelled quantities and shipped/returned balances. Edited quantity means the non-cancelled total including shipped units; dated delivery quantities sum only the outstanding balance. Shipping consumes earliest outstanding dates; direct cancellation removes latest dates first. The compatible order promise date is the latest remaining delivery date, while planning retains the individual dated pegs; when no delivery remains, the last approved promise date stays as history. Customer approval dates are real calendar dates no later than today in UTC. Price changes apply prospectively to unshipped quantities; picked shipment prices/addresses remain immutable. Picked/packed quantities require an explicit undo-pick before cancellation, and excess reservations are released without consuming any location’s picked commitment. Work and supplier commitments remain visible and require their own authorized change. Historical shipments missing an issued price are labelled incomplete; repricing, invoice values, returns or corrections requiring that missing price are refused pending source-document reconciliation, with no current-price substitution. **Acceptance evidence:** Increase, split-date and cancel the unshipped remainder of a partly produced order; inspect reservation/supply changes, retained shipment and incurred cost without deleting history.

- **DEMAND-003 — Master production schedule and forecasts.** Planners maintain time-bucketed sales forecasts and a master production schedule, import forecast CSV, distinguish actual orders from forecast consumption, and record overrides, seasonality and forecast error. Actual demand consumes forecasts rather than doubling them. Weeks start Monday at 00:00 UTC with an exclusive next-Monday end; quantities use each SKU's base unit. Baseline × nonnegative seasonality rounds half away from zero to six places, and an explicit nonnegative override replaces that result. Confirmed outstanding schedules and issued shipped-delivery quantities consume forecast for the same SKU/week; quotes and cancelled quantities do not. Remaining commercial demand is outstanding orders plus max(0, adjusted forecast minus actual ordered demand), with existing hard reservations kept separate. Error is actual ordered demand minus adjusted forecast, including fulfilled deliveries, rather than a claim of completed-period accuracy. Historical shipped quantities without issued delivery-date evidence are explicit and block MPS preparation pending source reconciliation. The MPS pools free available stock and existing draft/open supply by week (receipt on or before Sunday); suggestions cover only the remaining commercial quantity and carry unused common stock forward. This weekly quantity plan does not claim finite capacity or change customer promises. An authorized human releases the exact state-version preview to linked draft work/purchases; work release and purchase spending approval remain their existing separate doors. Manager/buyer/production/planner/agent prepare imports and MPS previews, manager/production/planner record reasoned seasonality/override changes and undo, and owner/manager/production/planner approve release (a different preparer except the owner); agents never release. Every original CSV/digest and before/after change remains retained, and undo appends the prior basis with its reason instead of deleting history. The GOV-001 planner role carries this forecast/MPS authority and separation. The location policy planner is the default for configured SKU/location sizing. The existing global DEMAND-003 MPS remains an explicit manual exception: its preview shows the effective policies and current catalog settings for suggested supply; release requires a human acknowledgment and recorded reason, retains the exact weekly quantities without automatic location sizing, and pins that policy evidence and decision on the plan and linked drafts. Existing independent human release authority still applies and agents cannot grant the exception. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a 12-week forecast, confirm overlapping sales orders and release an MPS; compare net demand and forecast error by week, then undo an override with history.

- **DEMAND-004 — Make-to-stock and make-to-order policies.** Each SKU/location selects make-to-stock, make-to-order or assemble-to-order with reorder point, safety stock, min/max, batch size, order multiple and lead time. Customer-specific work stays pegged while common stock supplies approved competing demand. The planner must honor ENG-007’s configured SKU/location reorder, safety, min/max, default-bin and lead-time values before shipping. Policies are immutable effective-dated SKU/location revisions maintained by manager/production people and GOV-001 planners. The current effective revision and current ENG-007 catalog location settings are read at preview; the default bin, or settings location when absent, is the physical pool and draft destination. Existing hard reservations remain protected across all bins. Approved common allocation considers explicitly selected confirmed orders and all still-open lines previously assigned to this bin, in rank/due-date order. The protected floor is max(safety stock, minimum stock); common available stock above it is shared only through exact reviewed allocations. For make-to-stock, projected inventory nets selected outstanding demand and located open/draft supply; at or below reorder it replenishes toward max(maximum stock, floor), otherwise only a floor shortfall is suggested. Make-to-order and assemble-to-order retain separate customer/order-line/date pegs for uncovered demand; assembly previews resolve the effective BOM and show shared component availability, shortage and late supply. Batch size and order multiple are base quantities; suggestions round upward to their least common exact six-place multiple. Lead time is calendar days in UTC, subtracted from required date; past starts and late supply are explicit, with no finite-capacity or customer-date claim. Located dedicated future supply stays with its exact order/line, and late supply nets quantity without claiming on-time coverage. Unlocated open supply blocks approval until a manager/production person or planner records its exact-version bin and optional customer peg without changing quantity, price or date. Existing assigned commitments cannot be silently relocated. A manager/buyer/production person, planner or agent prepares; owner/manager/production/planner approves an exact state-version preview, independently except the owner. Approval records planning-bin metadata, reserves only the displayed common available quantities and creates linked draft work or purchases; physical receipt/output, work release and spending retain their separate authorized doors. Earlier policy/preview/approval and demand pegs remain in the complete archive; current catalog edits invalidate old previews rather than rewriting them. The GOV-001 planner role carries this maintenance and independent approval authority. The location policy planner is the default for configured SKU/location sizing. The existing global DEMAND-003 MPS remains an explicit manual exception: its preview shows the effective policies and current catalog settings for suggested supply; release requires a human acknowledgment and recorded reason, retains the exact weekly quantities without automatic location sizing, and pins that policy evidence and decision on the plan and linked drafts. Existing independent human release authority still applies and agents cannot grant the exception. **Acceptance evidence:** Run the same demand with stock and order policies, approve the suggested batches and inspect customer pegging, safety stock and unused common inventory.

- **DEMAND-005 — Allocation priority and customer commitments.** Planners rank open sales and manufacturing orders explicitly (drag in the list or set a rank), and scarce supply is allocated in that rank with due-date and margin consequences. Reallocation shows displaced orders, requires approval and preserves hard-reservation integrity; customers receive only approved promise changes. Rank drives the live order status in DEMAND-006. **Acceptance evidence:** Allocate one receipt between two orders, preview displacement, approve a split and inspect both promises and reservation totals; re-rank two orders and see the allocation follow the new rank; deny a sales user authority to steal stock. Approve a machine-outage recovery with sufficient alternate capacity that preserves the original promises; read zero changed customer dates and inspect unchanged order dates. A recovery needing changed promises lists their count and old/new dates before the authorized person's approval.

- **DEMAND-006 — Live order material and production status.** The sales order list and the manufacturing order list show, for every order and line, an ingredient status (In stock, Expected with the date and reference of the supplying PO or MO, Not available, or No BOM) and a product/production status (not started, blocked, in progress, done, ready to ship). Status is allocated in the DEMAND-005 order rank, with protected hard reservations taking precedence over free-supply ranking, so a higher-ranked order is covered first from free supply, and is derived from current stock, quarantine, reservations, open POs and MOs, their current ETAs and rank. For a made sales-order line, finished stock and open-MO supply are consumed once; the quantity covered by an open MO inherits that MO's material coverage, and only the uncovered quantity is exploded through its effective BOM. MOs have an explicit, editable expectedOn completion date; an existing MO's requiredBy remains its declared supply date until changed. This date is an operational declared ETA, not proof of CAP finite scheduling. Both lists name every actual supply reference and date and recompute on each read. The next read after any receipt, ETA change, re-rank, reservation, cancellation or production report shows the recomputed status, without running MRP or any planning job. Both lists sort and filter by rank and status. **Acceptance evidence:** With three ranked orders competing for one component held partly in stock and partly on two POs, (a) part-receive one PO, (b) slip the other PO's ETA and (c) swap the rank of two orders. After each step, with no planning run, the SO and MO lists (screen at 1440 and 390 pixels, and the state API) show the correct In stock / Expected-with-date / Not available status for each order and line; every Expected date equals the supplying PO's or MO's current date, and a quarantined receipt never shows as In stock. Cover a made SO line partly with finished stock and an open MO, verify those supplies are consumed once and the covered quantity inherits the MO's material coverage, then verify only the uncovered quantity uses its effective BOM. Re-rank competing demand and confirm protected hard reservations still take precedence over free supply. The plan and the pick judge eligibility on the order's promise date or today, whichever is later; a pick takes a named eligible lot or eligible lots in FEFO order net of quantities already picked into unshipped shipments, and dispatch rechecks expiry, retest and the customer minimum on the current UTC day. Expired or retest-due available stock stays on hand but is excluded from availability, reservation, planning supply, shipment, issue and transfer; counts still record it. Today alerts each lot with stock expiring within 30 days (acknowledgeable) and each expired or retest-due lot with stock (cleared only by its disposition). Only the owner, a manager or a GOV-001 quality-role holder records the documented disposition, with a reason and at least one evidence file: extend after a passed retest (new expiry/retest dates after today that unblock the lot) or scrap (the lot's available stock at one location written off as a compensating movement); agents and other roles cannot. Each decision retains the before/after dates, evidence digests, person and time.
